Jatoi · Radar-change verification targets

Ten largest interior HH change-supported clusters identified from NASA–ISRO NISAR 12 Aug → 5 Sep 2026 observations. Points show estimated connected-component centres only; they are not measured inundation boundaries.

Research candidates — not confirmed floods. These points are selected from a radar change screening rule (post-date HH ≤−18 dB and HH darkening ≥3 dB), excluding JRC historical occurrence ≥25% for sensitivity analysis. JRC ≥25% is not a permanent-water definition. Areas are approximate 20 m-cell counts. Independent scene-level optical or field verification at these exact coordinates remains outstanding.

Choose a centre to inspect it on the map. Red markers are all unverified. The source raster remains in the team's Google Drive and is not represented by these pins.

Why not use this as a flood alert? HH darkness can indicate water, wet soil, river sandbar dynamics, agriculture, layover or acquisition artifacts. A mapped regional flood report does not verify these specific cells. The 4 Sep Copernicus GFM reference is satellite-derived and one day before the 5 Sep NISAR post-image.
