# Concise demo — approximately 30 seconds (rehearse at your normal pace)

> We built Under the Clouds to test what radar surface changes really mean. Using real NISAR observations, we mapped 290 exploratory change clusters in Jatoi, Pakistan — about 0.57 square kilometres. We checked those candidates against Sentinel-2 and Copernicus flood references, but flooding was not independently confirmed. Our 150-day optical calendar distinguishes 56 usable observations, 12 obstructed observations, and 82 days without imagery. This bilingual tool shows both the evidence and its limits, and exports candidate locations for field verification.

Show, in order: homepage → radar map → Blind Days calendar → validation caveat → CSV export. If the official event requires a different length, use that requirement instead.
